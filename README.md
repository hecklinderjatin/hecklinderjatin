## 👾 &nbsp;Who am I?

Hey, I'm Jatin. I'm a 4th-year Computer Engineering undergrad who likes building things from scratch rather than relying on black boxes. Whether I'm writing neural networks raw in NumPy, designing 3D web experiences, or troubleshooting `libalpm` conflicts on my Arch machine, I build out of pure curiosity.

When I'm not in an IDE, I'm probably out for a 6km run, playing Catan: Cities & Knights, or catching up on the F1 season.
